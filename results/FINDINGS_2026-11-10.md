## Preliminary findings (subject to change after adding time-of-day)
1. **Baseline is already strong.** Delay + turnaround alone: AUC 0.90, PR-AUC 0.88, Brier 0.143 in 2020 (constant-rate reference 0.272).
2. **Weather + traffic do not help the logistic model.** Brier(expanded) − Brier(baseline): +0.0041 (2020), +0.0006 (2021), −0.0009 (2022); all 95% day-bootstrap CIs exclude zero. Net effect over 2020–22 is slightly negative (+0.0008).
3. **The penalty is largest in low-visibility conditions** (LIFR: +0.0062, CI 0.0036–0.0090). A linear weather term is a poor description of how weather acts.
4. **XGBoost beats logistic regression** (Brier 0.121 vs 0.143 in 2020) and expanded-XGBoost beats baseline-XGBoost in 2021 (0.1275 vs 0.1287) and 2022 (0.1335 vs 0.1356), but not 2020. Weather/traffic carry a small, nonlinear signal.
5. **COVID shift.** Recovery rates rose 10–20 points in 2020 and declined afterwards. 2019-trained models still rank well in 2020 (AUC ≈ 0.90) but calibration must be checked (figures/reliability.png). PR-AUC falls 0.88 → 0.80 → 0.78 across 2020–22, partly because the base rate falls.
6. **Time of day and delay size dominate.** Recovery: 68% (00–05h), 56% (06–11h), 41% (12–17h), 34% (18–23h). 61% for 15–30 min delays vs 20% for >120 min. High scheduled traffic (top quartile) lowers recovery to 38% vs 43% at low traffic.
7. **Leave-one-airport-out:** weather helps only when ATL is held out (−0.0033); it hurts for DEN, ORD, SEA, SFO. Weather effects do not transfer across airports.

## Known issues
- AUC printed as NA for large groups (integer overflow in `auc_roc`); fixed with `as.numeric()`.
- `glm.fit` warning about fitted probabilities of 0/1: harmless (extreme delays).
- **Hour of day is not yet a predictor.** Because it is strongly related to recovery and to weather, today's weather results may partly reflect time-of-day confounding. Must be added to both models before the findings are final.
